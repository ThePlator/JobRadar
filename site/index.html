Blip reads the job channels, so you don't have to.

JobRadar is a small agent you run on your own computer. It watches your Telegram job channels and the company job boards you pick, keeps the jobs that fit you, emails you the strong ones, and writes a one-page resume that only says true things.

Blip's red pen

Before a tailored resume is built, Blip checks every AI rewrite against the bullet it came from. A new number, a "10x", a tool you never mentioned, or a word like "expert", and your own sentence goes back in.

    Wake up your own Blip

    You'll need Python 3.11 or newer, uv, a Telegram account, a Notion database made from the template and a free Gemini key. Your keys go in .env, which never leaves your machine.

    Click a step to see what it prints on a first run. Blip sleeps until the last one.

    Every command is explained in docs/COMMANDS.md.

    1. Cloning into 'JobRadar'...
    2. Installs Python packages into .venv; nothing global.
    3. + created config/config.yaml from config.example.yaml: edit it before continuing+ created config/profile.yaml from profile.example.yaml: edit it before continuing+ created .env from .env.example: edit it before continuingTelegram login (a code is sent to your Telegram app; nothing is posted):✓ Telegram logged in as you✓ Notion database found and matches the template
    4. Lists the Telegram chats you can read, with their ids.Put ids (or @usernames) under sources.telegram.chats in config.yaml.
    5. Blip is awake. Leave this running; Ctrl+C puts it back to sleep.
