A little agent that gets you to the job first.

JobRadar reads your Telegram job channels and the company job boards you pick, scores every posting against your profile, emails you the strong ones about a minute after they go up, and writes a one-page resume that never makes things up.

Hi! I'm Blip. Point me at a channel.

Good jobs get reposted. The repost comes a day and a half late.

In my own logs, when a job showed up in more than one Telegram channel, the last copy arrived a median of 35 hours after the first. Fresh applications get read first, so as a fresher I was always one of the late ones. I built JobRadar so the first copy reaches me, not the last.

  1. Watch

    Blip reads every new post in your Telegram job channels the moment it arrives, and checks the company job boards you list (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters) every 20 minutes. My setup watches 40 boards.

  2. Clean

    Every link becomes one job. The same job posted in three channels is one row that says "Seen in 3". Course ads and promo links are hidden, and Blip asks the hiring site whether a posting is still open before spending any effort on it.

  3. Score

    Blip reads the details from the job page and the post itself, and only asks Gemini when those don't say enough. Then each job gets a Match Score against your profile: batch, branch, skills, location and seniority, with the reason written next to it.

  4. Alert

    A strong, fresh match lands in your inbox as soon as it's scored. Everything else waits in a Notion inbox and a twice-daily digest. When you mark a job Applied or Interview in Notion, Blip notices and never touches it again.

  5. Resume

    Shortlist a job and Blip writes a one-page resume for it in your own LaTeX layout, choosing and ordering your real bullets. A checker undoes any rewrite that adds a number, a tool or a buzzword you never wrote.

It won't make things up

An AI rewrite is checked against the bullet it claims to rewrite. If it adds a number, a size word like "doubled", a technology, or a buzzword from the banned list, your own wording goes back in. Try three rewrites of one real-looking bullet.

Under the hood

One Python process on your machine. Every step is a task in a small SQLite queue, so a crash or a restart never loses a post, and every step can be retried.

Run your own Blip

You need Python 3.11 or newer, uv, a Telegram account, a Notion database and a free Gemini key. Setup asks for each one.

  1. git clone https://github.com/ThePlator/JobRadar && cd JobRadar
  2. uv sync
  3. uv run jobradar init
  4. uv run jobradar run

Every command is explained in docs/COMMANDS.md.
